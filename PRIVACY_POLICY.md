# Privacy Policy — Noor-A-Zakat

*Last updated: 5 November 2026*

**Developer / Contact:** [APNA NAAM] — [APNA EMAIL]

Noor-A-Zakat ("app") aapki privacy ka khayal rakhta hai.

## Jo data app aapke device par rakhta hai
Aapka naam, umar, zewar/sampatti/qarz ka hisab aur Zakat kist ka record **sirf aapke phone mein**, Android Keystore ke zariye encrypt karke rakha jata hai. Hum ye data apne server par **nahi bhejte** aur na hi hamari tak pahunchta hai.

## Internet ka istemal
- **Live bhav:** sone/chandi ka international bhav aur currency rate lene ke liye app third-party public APIs (gold-api.com, open.er-api.com) ko request bhejta hai. Is request mein aapki personal jaankari shamil nahi hoti, lekin aapka IP address in services ko dikh sakta hai.

## Ads (Google AdMob)
App Google AdMob ke zariye ads dikhata hai. AdMob aur Google aapke device ki jaankari (jaise Advertising ID, IP address, device/app usage) ads dikhane, fraud rokne aur measurement ke liye istemal kar sakte hain. EEA/UK/Switzerland mein ads dikhane se pehle aapki consent (Google UMP) li jati hai, aur aap Settings > "Ads Privacy Options" se apni pasand badal sakte hain.
Details: https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads

## Bachchon ki privacy
App 13 saal se kam umar ke bachchon ko jaan-boojhkar nishana nahi banata. Ads family-friendly (G) content rating tak mehdood rakhi gayi hain.

## Data hatana
App ka data hatane ke liye: Android Settings > Apps > Noor-A-Zakat > Storage > Clear data, ya app uninstall karein. App ke andar Settings mein "Reset" se hisab mita sakte hain.
